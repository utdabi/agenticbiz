Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 17, 2026  ·  Applies to the PromptMate browser extension for Chrome and Firefox.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry.
  • All your data (PII terms, prompts, settings) stays on your device in browser storage.
  • When you use the Prompt Enhancer, your text goes directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API — it never passes through our servers.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes everything.

1. Who we are

PromptMate is a browser extension developed and maintained as an independent project. We do not operate servers that process or store user data.

2. What data is stored — and where

PromptMate stores the following data exclusively in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). This storage is local to your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Data Purpose Leaves your device?
Toggle states (masking on/off, enhancer on/off) Remember your preferences No
Custom PII terms Used locally to mask text before paste No
Prompt library Your saved reusable prompts No
Enhancement template Your customised system prompt for Gemini No — except as part of Gemini API calls you initiate
Allowed sites list Controls which domains PII masking is active on No
Gemini API key Authenticates requests to Google's Gemini API Only to Google (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) when you trigger enhancement

3. The Prompt Enhancer and Google Gemini Third party

If you choose to use the Prompt Enhancer feature, you supply your own Gemini API key. When you right-click and select "Enhance prompt", your browser sends a request containing:

  • The text from the active input field
  • Your enhancement template
  • Your API key (as a URL parameter, per Google's API design)

This request goes directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com). It does not pass through any PromptMate infrastructure. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Gemini API Terms of Service.

The Prompt Enhancer is entirely opt-in and requires you to provide your own API key. You can disable it at any time in settings.

4. PII masking is entirely local

The PII masking feature operates exclusively within your browser. Text is processed by the content script running on the page — no data is sent anywhere. Your custom PII terms never leave your device.

5. No analytics or tracking

PromptMate does not include analytics, crash reporting, or any form of usage tracking. We have no visibility into how, when, or where you use the extension.

6. Permissions we request and why

Permission Why it's needed
storage Save your settings, PII terms, and prompt library locally on your device
contextMenus Add the right-click "Enhance prompt" menu item
<all_urls> (content script) Run the PII masking and prompt insertion features on any website you visit — the extension cannot function if restricted to specific domains
generativelanguage.googleapis.com Allow the background service worker to call the Gemini API on your behalf when you trigger enhancement

7. Data retention and deletion

All data stored by PromptMate is retained only in your browser's local storage. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Clearing your custom PII terms or prompt library from the Settings page, or
  • Uninstalling the extension — this permanently removes all locally stored data

8. Children's privacy

PromptMate is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and, where possible, note the change in the extension's release notes. Continued use of the extension after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
